Tabular demand forecast with a frozen evaluation protocol

Compares a pre-declared gradient-boosting grid with three fixed baselines under a frozen synthetic evaluation. On the retained test, GBM has lower MAE than seasonal-naive in every reported stratum, but loses to naive-last-week on the unseen store's shifted SKUs and has the largest negative bias. These are descriptive results on this synthetic set, not evidence of real-world forecasting performance.
